street probe

give it a coordinate — it reports every street-level panorama within the radius, and opens any of them in 360. costs nothing.
Click the map to move the point. Keys persist to localStorage only.

Nothing on this page can be billed. The panorama walk (StreetViewService), the availability checks (metadata), and the 360 viewer (Embed API) are all unmetered. The one billable call in Street View — the Static image endpoint — is never requested, which is why Google results show a year rather than a thumbnail. Mapillary is free throughout.
